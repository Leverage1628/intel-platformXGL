# MERIDIAN

**A strategic intelligence dashboard tracking 59 conflict and pressure theaters across the world — and the connections between them.**

MERIDIAN is a single, self-contained analytical instrument. It opens in any browser, works with no connection, and holds a complete picture of the world's active fault lines in one place: the wars, the standoffs, the economic chokeholds, and the armed actors moving through all of them.

It is built on one conviction — that most intelligence products tell you *what happened* and stop there. MERIDIAN is built to show *how the pieces hold together*: why a strait closing in one hemisphere moves a currency in another, why a rebellion in one country tracks a neighbour's export figures, and why the same playbook keeps reappearing under different flags.

---

## What it covers

**59 theaters**, from the highest-intensity wars to the slow-burning pressure points that rarely make headlines until they break.

**103 armed actors**, each profiled with its own capabilities, economics, and strategic assessment — from national armies to clan militias to private military contractors.

**19 analytical boards**, cutting across the theaters by theme: sanctions, sovereign debt, information operations, maritime chokepoints, the humanitarian ledger, great-power patronage, and more.

**A structured spillover layer** connecting 35 theaters through 136 documented cross-border links — mapping what actually crosses each border, and in which direction.

Coverage spans every major region: the Sahel and the Great Lakes, the Horn and North Africa, the Middle East and the Gulf, Eastern Europe and the Caucasus, South and Southeast Asia, the Pacific, and the Americas.

---

## What makes it different

**It holds contradictions instead of resolving them.** Where the evidence genuinely supports two readings, MERIDIAN shows both and says so. A state can be a development success *and* a documented sponsor of a rebellion *and* a mineral-trade beneficiary at once — and the platform is built to keep all three on the screen rather than flattening them into a headline.

**Every claim carries its provenance.** Each armed-actor profile is tagged by how well its current status is verified. Nothing is presented as more certain than it is.

**It grades its own predictions.** The platform maintains a set of forward-looking tripwires — specific, dated things to watch. When the window passes, each one is scored against what actually happened, *including the ones it got wrong*. The count of graded-versus-outstanding is shown on the platform's face.

**It marks its own blind spots.** Where a theater has no armed groups, or a border has no spillover, or a dispute has no documented sides, the platform says the space is deliberately empty rather than quietly leaving a gap. An honest absence is treated as a finding.

**It corrects itself in the open.** When new reporting overturns something the platform had recorded, the correction is logged in place — never silently overwritten. The record of what was believed, and when it changed, is part of the product.

---

## The lens

MERIDIAN reads the world through a consistent analytical question: **who controls what a place's resources are for, and who pays the price of that control.** It follows the money and the minerals as closely as the front lines — because the security arrangement and the extraction arrangement are usually the same arrangement, seen from two sides.

That lens is applied evenly. It is as interested in the internal periphery that funds a war as in the external power that profits from one, and it names the pattern wherever it appears rather than only where it is fashionable to look.

---

## Using it

Open the file in any browser. No installation, no account, no connection required. Every board is reachable from the top navigation; every theater card expands into its full history, competing interpretations, sourcing, and cross-border links.

The whole thing is one file by design — so it can be archived, carried, or read offline as a complete snapshot of a moment in the world.

---

*MERIDIAN is an analytical synthesis of open-source reporting. It is a tool for thinking about how the world's pressure points connect — not an official or authoritative record. Read it as one carefully-constructed argument about the shape of the whole, and follow its sources to check any part.*
